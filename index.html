Aleator: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Aleator is a card game for Android and iOS published by Elanto ("we"). This page explains what information is handled when you play it.

What the game itself collects

Nothing that identifies you. Aleator has no accounts and no sign-in, and we run no servers that receive your data. Your settings, statistics, achievements and unfinished run are stored only on your device, and are removed when you uninstall the game.

Advertising

The free game shows adverts supplied by Google AdMob. To deliver and measure them, Google and its advertising partners may collect and process:

  • device identifiers, including your device's advertising ID;
  • your IP address, from which an approximate location can be worked out;
  • information about your device and the app (model, operating system, language, app version);
  • which adverts you were shown and how you interacted with them;
  • diagnostic information, such as crash and performance data from the advertising software.

This information goes directly from your device to Google. We do not receive it in a form that identifies you. How Google uses it is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in Google's Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the game asks for your consent before personalised adverts are shown. You can change your answer at any time under Settings > Privacy choices in the game.
  • On iOS, you may be asked whether the app may track your activity. You can change this at any time in the iOS Settings app, under Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in the Android Settings app, under Privacy > Ads.
  • Buying the game removes all adverts. Once it is bought, the game no longer starts the advertising software.

Purchases

The one-time purchase is handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see your payment details. The game only learns from the store whether the purchase was made, and remembers that on your device.

Children

Aleator is a card game with a casino theme and is not intended for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects your privacy, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change.

Contact

Questions or requests about privacy in Aleator can be sent by opening an issue at github.com/Mario-El-Antoury/aleator-privacy/issues.
